Privacy Statement

At SAMJHO, we believe that the right to information should not come at the cost of your personal privacy. Unlike most financial education platforms, SAMJHO does not require a login, registration, or any form of personal identification. You are free to explore the "Dark Realities" of the banking system with total anonymity.

What We (Don't) Collect: Since there is no account creation, we do not maintain a central database of our users. We do not know who you are. We may collect minimal, non-identifiable technical data—such as your device type and app interaction patterns—purely to optimize our content. This data cannot be traced back to any individual.

Data Integrity: Even without personal data collection, we adhere to the principles of the DPDP Act of India. Our "Zero-Data Footprint" ensures there is no information on our servers to compromise. We do not use cross-site tracking cookies, nor do we sell user behavior to the institutions we expose.

Your Experience: Any preferences or "Saved Shorts" are stored locally on your device. By using SAMJHO, you acknowledge that you are accessing a platform built on trust. We are here to give you the truth about the system—without tracking your every move. Your privacy is our mission.
